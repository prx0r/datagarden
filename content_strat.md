# Content Strategy — Clickbait questions, non-clickbait answers

## The operating principle

> **Start with the title people cannot ignore. Then build the data pipeline required to answer it truthfully.**

That flips the normal process.

Instead of:

```text
collect data
→ see what charts are possible
→ invent content
```

do:

```text
irresistible money question
→ define exact metric needed
→ collect/transform only the data required
→ answer immediately
→ show evidence
→ repeat daily
```

---

## The editorial rule

> **No suspense farming. Give the answer in the first 10 seconds, then prove it.**

> "The UK jobs getting crowded fastest"

> "The fastest crowding we found this week is X, Y and Z. X is especially bad because entrant growth is outrunning demand by __. Here's the data."

## The forcing function

> **If we cannot define a reliable metric behind the title, we don't make the video.**

Every title maps to a metric:

```text
"The UK jobs getting crowded fastest"
→ entrant growth / demand growth

"Things people are selling too cheaply"
→ ask price / fair value

"Fastest-selling second-hand products"
→ median listing survival / sale time

"Most profitable network"
→ reward per unit resource after costs
```

## The content/data loop

$$
\boxed{\text{Title} \rightarrow \text{Metric} \rightarrow \text{Collector} \rightarrow \text{Answer}}
$$

The accumulated metrics become the moat.

Because the answer is useful immediately, the audience has a reason to come back rather than just being entertained.

---

## The brand promise

> **Clickbait questions, non-clickbait answers.**

That differentiates from generic YouTube sludge.

The titles become the research backlog.

If we can't define the metric, we don't make the video.

If we can, we build the collector.

Then the collector feeds the garden.

Then the garden produces the next answer.

---

## The loop

```text
TITLE (irresistible question)
    ↓
METRIC (exact measurement needed)
    ↓
COLLECTOR (data pipeline)
    ↓
ANSWER (immediate, evidence-backed)
    ↓
AUDIENCE (comes back for more answers)
    ↓
FEEDBACK (what questions do they have?)
    ↓
BETTER METRICS
    ↓
BETTER DATA
    ↓
BETTER ANSWERS
    ↓
TITLE
```

Every circuit leaves behind permanent history.

That's the garden.
