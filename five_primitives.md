# The Five Primitive Gardens

## The realization

Most gardens can be reduced to five primitive gardens.

---

## The five primitives

### 1. Want Garden

What do people want?

### 2. Pain Garden

What is currently difficult/expensive/broken?

### 3. Price Garden

What is something really worth?

### 4. Supply Garden

Who can provide it, where and at what capacity?

### 5. Change Garden

What just altered the equilibrium?

---

## The useful thing comes from joins

Example:

```text
Want ↑
+
Supply flat
+
Price ↑
+
regulation creates demand
        ↓
OPPORTUNITY
```

That is basically the Seesaw expressed as data infrastructure.

## Crops

A crop could literally be:

```text
plumbers × Manchester
```

or:

```text
accountants × AI software complaints
```

or:

```text
EV chargers × Kent
```

Then cultivate it continuously.

---

## The most powerful question

For every crop:

> **What does this group want, what hurts, what do they currently pay, what supply exists, and what has just changed?**

That produces actual economic fruit rather than generic trend content.

---

## The deepest version

You are building sensors for unmet demand, then letting better and better frontier intelligence harvest opportunities from the accumulated history.

---

## Why this matters

It gives a clean architecture for all projects.

Every garden is some combination of Want + Pain + Price + Supply + Change.

The joins between them are where the value lives.

And each primitive can be planted independently, then connected later.

That means you can start with one tiny crop and grow.
