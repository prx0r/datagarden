# Plant the Trees

## The immediate insight

Make it **super clear this is the reason you are clicking on this video right now**.

Then have it as a hypothesis: given all our data, here's what we think will hit well and why.

All of this leads to higher-order moats which we can discover later.

**What we need to do now is plant the trees.**

---

## The hypothesis engine

Every video is an experiment:

```text
HYPOTHESIS:
This title + this answer will perform well because
[audience signal] + [data novelty] + [economic relevance]

PREDICTION:
CTR > X%
30s retention > Y%
saves > Z

OUTCOME:
actual CTR
actual retention
actual saves

LEARNING:
what worked, what didn't, what to grow
```

---

## The "why click now" principle

Every video must answer immediately:

> **Why should you care about this TODAY?**

Not:
> "Here's an interesting dataset."

Instead:
> "This changed yesterday. Here's what it means for your money right now."

Examples:

> "New regulation just created 1,200 jobs in heat pumps. Here's where."

> "This product dropped 40% on eBay this week. Here's why."

> "Three trades just crossed the crowding threshold. Here's which ones."

Urgency is the hook.

Data is the proof.

- Prune what didn't work

---

## The beauty

Higher-order moats will emerge later.

We don't need to design them now.

We need to plant the trees and let the audience tell us which ones to water.

$$
\boxed{\text{Plant now. Grow later. Let the audience decide.}}
$$
