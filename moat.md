# The Moat Crystallized

## The moat equation

$$
\boxed{\text{unique transformation} \times \text{continuous collection} \times \text{time}}
$$

Not raw data.

## The hard rule

> **Only collect data when we can continuously transform it into a proprietary measurement worth preserving.**

---

## The whole thesis

PowPowPow transforms fragmented resource/market telemetry into **compute economics**.

Breadup transforms fragmented marketplaces into **physical-object economics**.

ROOM transforms fragmented geographic/economic signals into **market capacity/crowding**.

The content and APIs are simply ways to consume those proprietary time series.

That's the whole thesis.
