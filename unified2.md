# Unified 2 — The Economy of Functions

## The right abstraction

No longer "a bunch of repos." An **economy of reusable functions** that can be composed into businesses, media properties, data products, agents, and consumer apps.

The useful unit is not the repo. It is the function.

---

## The six things

1. **Observe reality**
2. **Transform it into something useful**
3. **Package that into an outlet**
4. **Put it in front of humans/agents**
5. **Measure what they do**
6. **Use that response to decide what to build/measure next**

That same loop explains Breadup, UKGraph, PowPowPow, roast.pet, FreakTown, Influence, EtsySignal, PogTown, plugins, APIs, and product discovery.

---

## The big insight

These are not separate startups. They are **different compositions of the same underlying functions**.
